# Workflow Atlas

Portable workflow rules live in the `ub-workflow` skill. This atlas routes project-local workflow artifacts to their owning files.

## Owners

- `vision.md`: product north star.
- `options.md`: curated product-level, future-wave, and unknown-owner options before commitment.
- `status.md`: current posture, active pointers, WIP, blockers, and next action.
- `waves/wNN-*/`: wave, initiative, local options, forecast, discovery, sprint, and evidence owners.
- `source-packs/YYYY-MM-DD-*/`: retained context only.
